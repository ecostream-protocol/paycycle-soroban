# Code of Conduct

We want EcoStream to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or experience.

**Expected behaviour**
- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.

**Unacceptable behaviour**
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or submitting work you did not do as your own.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who break these rules. Report problems by email to the address in the repository's profile, or by opening a private security advisory. Reports are handled in confidence.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
